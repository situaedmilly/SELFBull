# MODELSELFWITNESS × SELFMOAT × PEEPDASUITE v0.1

Status: CANDIDATE DOCTRINE
Origin: ACT-SELFBULL-V0.1-MODELSELFWITNESS-SELFMOAT-PEEPDASUITE-FORMATION-001

## Distinction

A foreign organism changing is not equivalent to SELF causing that change.

`FOREIGN_EVOLUTION != SELF_REGRESSION`

SELF does not require its environment to remain frozen. Every external change must cross an explicit epistemic membrane before becoming SELF's admitted reality.

## MODELSELFWITNESS

MODELSELFWITNESS replaces a mutable-working-tree-only witness with a relational witness:

`ForeignReality -> Witness -> Delta -> Impact -> Admission`

Minimum dimensions:

- Identity: repository identity, canonical path, protected artifact.
- Admission: admitted commit, blob, digest, receipt, reason.
- Observation: current commit, blob, worktree digest and custody state.
- Lineage: ancestry, commits since admission, artifact change.
- Causality: SELF_CAUSED, FOREIGN_LAWFUL_EVOLUTION, FOREIGN_UNADMITTED_EVOLUTION, PREEXISTING_DRIFT, UNKNOWN_CAUSALITY.
- Semantic delta: authority, interface and behavioral impact.
- Standing: derived from the dimensions above. `overall` MUST NOT be authored or defaulted.

### Identity law

Filesystem SHA-256 remains evidence of physical bytes but is subordinate to versioned identity.

`Witness = RepoIdentity + CommitIdentity + ArtifactPath + BlobIdentity + WorktreeDigest + AdmissionReceipt`

Git object identity and filesystem digest are complementary, not substitutes.

## SELFMOAT

SELFMOAT is the causal-sovereignty boundary between an observing SELF and independently evolving foreign organisms.

Foreign evolution is observable but never inheritable by default.

Minimum standings:

- UNCHANGED
- LAWFULLY_EVOLVED
- EVOLVED_REQUIRES_READMISSION
- LOCAL_CONTAMINATION
- IDENTITY_MISMATCH
- LINEAGE_DIVERGENCE
- CAUSALITY_UNKNOWN
- WITNESS_INVALID

Hard invariant:

`UNKNOWN_CAUSALITY != FOREIGN_LAWFUL_EVOLUTION`

Unknown causality MUST HOLD or REFUSE. It MUST NOT silently admit foreign evolution.

## PEEPDASUITE

PEEPDASUITE is the pressure instrument for test topology. A suite result MUST distinguish failure jurisdiction instead of reducing the whole reality to one Boolean.

Minimum suite species:

- SELF
- BOUNDARY
- FOREIGN_WITNESS
- ENVIRONMENT

A failure should establish, where evidence permits:

- owner and jurisdiction
- expected and observed state
- temporal origin
- causal origin
- self regression
- foreign drift
- environmental drift
- witness defect
- mutation relevance
- whether local correctness is blocked
- whether repository seal is blocked
- whether the next gate is blocked
- disposition

Therefore a valid state may be:

`LOCAL_CORRECTNESS = GREEN`

`FOREIGN_WITNESS = RED`

`REPOSITORY_SEAL = BLOCKED`

without falsely asserting `SELF_REGRESSION = TRUE`.

## Cross-organism law

This distinction generalizes across the ecosystem:

- SELFBULL witnesses SELFQUANT and Webull SDK/API state.
- HTMLSELF may witness design runtimes.
- UREEL may witness Unreal/runtime versions.
- AGENT BRIDGE may witness protocol/kernel state.
- REEFREQUENCY may witness market-provider state.
- OURSELF may witness external SDKs and independently evolving organs.

The relationship is always explicit:

`ForeignReality -> Witness -> Delta -> Impact -> Admission`

Never:

`dependency changed -> everything red`

and never:

`dependency changed -> silently inherited`

## Seal law

A foreign witness may block repository seal or next-gate eligibility without falsifying local implementation correctness.

No stale hash may be refreshed merely to manufacture green.

No foreign evolution may be promoted without explicit admission evidence.

This doctrine is intentionally independent of any single SELFQUANT hash incident. The incident exposed the distinction; it does not define the distinction.
